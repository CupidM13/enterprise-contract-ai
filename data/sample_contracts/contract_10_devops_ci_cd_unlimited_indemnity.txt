CLOUDBUILD RUNNER SYSTEMS, INC.

1. CI/CD HOSTED RUNNERS

Invoices are due within fifteen (15) days of receipt ("Net 15"). Late payments accrue interest at three percent (3.0%) per month.

The Agreement term is twelve (12) months. The Agreement auto-renews for consecutive one-year terms unless either party submits written non-renewal notice at least fourteen (14) days prior to the conclusion of the active term. No convenience termination is permitted.

4. UPTIME AND SERVICE LEVELS
Provider provides hosted runners on a commercially reasonable efforts basis, targeting 99.0% availability during US business hours. Provider does not issue financial service credits for failed build executions or runner downtime.
