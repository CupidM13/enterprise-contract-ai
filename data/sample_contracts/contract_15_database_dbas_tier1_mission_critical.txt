MISSION-CRITICAL DISTRIBUTED CLOUD DATABASE (DBAAS) AGREEMENT
QUANTUMSCALE DISTRIBUTED DATABASE SYSTEMS INC.

This Master Cloud Database License and Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of February 1, 2027, by and between QuantumScale Distributed Database Systems Inc., a Delaware corporation ("QuantumScale" or "Vendor"), and Enterprise Client Corp. ("Customer").

1. MISSION-CRITICAL CLOUD DATABASE LICENSING
Vendor delivers dedicated multi-region distributed SQL and vector database clusters. The Annual Contract Value is Four Hundred Eighty Thousand US Dollars ($480,000.00 USD), invoiced on a semi-annual schedule.

2. COMMERCIAL TERMS AND INVOICE SETTLEMENT
Customer shall pay all undisputed invoice amounts within sixty (60) calendar days from receipt of invoice ("Net 60"). Late payments are assessed simple interest at one-half percent (0.5%) per month. Customer may withhold good-faith disputed sums without service disruption.

3. DURATION AND RENEWAL NOTIFICATION
The initial term shall run for thirty-six (36) months from service commencement. This Agreement renews for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) calendar days prior to expiration. Customer may terminate for convenience upon thirty (30) days prior written notice, subject to pro-rata reimbursement of unearned prepaid fees.

4. HIGH AVAILABILITY SLA & PENALTY CREDITS
QuantumScale warrants a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least ninety-nine and ninety-nine hundredths percent (99.99%) across distributed storage nodes. If uptime falls below 99.99%, Customer is entitled to a graduated service credit of twenty-five percent (25%) of monthly fees, and fifty percent (50%) if uptime drops below 99.90%, refundable in cash upon contract conclusion.

5. LIABILITY LIMITATION AND COMPREHENSIVE CARVE-OUTS
(a) Except as set forth in Section 5(b), each party's maximum cumulative liability shall be capped at two times (2.0x) the fees paid or payable by Customer in the preceding twelve (12) months ($960,000.00 USD).
(b) Section 5(a) shall not apply to: (i) gross negligence or willful misconduct; (ii) breach of Section 7 (Confidentiality and Customer Data); or (iii) indemnification obligations regarding third-party intellectual property claims.

6. DATA SOVEREIGNTY AND ENCRYPTION STANDARDS
All Customer Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 bit encryption keys. All primary and replica clusters reside exclusively within the United States. Vendor maintains an annual AICPA SOC 2 Type II audit attestation covering Security, Availability, and Confidentiality. Security breach notifications are delivered in writing within twenty-four (24) hours of verification.

7. BURIED SUBPROCESSOR RSS NOTIFICATION CLAUSE
Section 7.3 (Subprocessor Syndication): Vendor publishes notice of newly onboarded cloud hosting and storage subprocessors exclusively via an authenticated RSS syndication feed. Customer failure to object via certified mail within five (5) business days of RSS posting constitutes irrevocable deemed consent to the subprocessor appointment.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, with dispute venue in New Castle County, Delaware.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first above written.
