CLINICAL ANALYTICS & HEALTHCARE PLATFORM AGREEMENT
MEDIPULSE CLINICAL ANALYTICS CORP.

This Healthcare Platform License and Business Associate Agreement ("Agreement") is dated August 1, 2026, by and between MediPulse Clinical Analytics Corp., a Delaware corporation ("MediPulse" or "Vendor"), and Enterprise Client Corp. ("Customer").

1. HEALTHCARE ANALYTICS PLATFORM & BAA
MediPulse provides HIPAA-compliant patient cohort analytics and clinical workflow software. The Annual Contract Value ("ACV") is Four Hundred Twenty Thousand US Dollars ($420,000.00 USD), invoiced on a semi-annual schedule.

2. PAYMENT TERMS AND INVOICE DISPUTES
Customer shall pay all undisputed invoice amounts within forty-five (45) calendar days from invoice presentation ("Net 45"). Vendor provides a settlement discount of two percent (2%) for invoices cleared within ten (10) days ("2% Net 10"). Undisputed overdue balances accrue interest at one percent (1.0%) per month. Customer may withhold disputed fees in good faith without penalty or threat of service suspension.

3. TERM, NOTICE WINDOW, AND CONVENIENCE RIGHTS
(a) The initial term shall be twenty-four (24) months. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives written notice of termination at least forty-five (45) days prior to the expiration date.
(b) Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days prior written notice, subject to a pro-rata reimbursement of unearned prepaid licensing fees.

4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT & CREDITS
MediPulse guarantees ninety-nine and ninety-five hundredths percent (99.95%) monthly uptime. In any calendar month where uptime drops below 99.95%, Customer shall receive a fifteen percent (15%) service credit against upcoming invoices, or a direct cash rebate upon contract termination.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY AND HIPAA CARVE-OUTS
(a) Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed two times (2.0x) the fees paid by Customer in the preceding twelve (12) months ($840,000.00 USD).
(b) The limitation in Section 5(a) shall NOT apply to: (i) gross negligence or intentional misconduct; (ii) breach of Section 6 (Confidentiality and Protected Health Information); or (iii) indemnification obligations under Section 9 for third-party intellectual property infringement.

6. DATA PRIVACY, HIPAA, AND BREACH DISCLOSURE
Vendor maintains active HITRUST CSF and AICPA SOC 2 Type II audit attestations. All Protected Health Information (PHI) and Customer Data shall be encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 bit encryption. All data centers reside exclusively within the United States. In the event of a confirmed or suspected Security Incident or Breach of Unsecured PHI, Vendor shall notify Customer in writing within twenty-four (24) hours.

7. SUBPROCESSORS
Vendor will provide Customer with thirty (30) days prior written notice before onboarding any new subprocessor. Customer may object to such subprocessor on reasonable compliance grounds within thirty (30) days of receipt of notice.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed under the laws of the State of Delaware.
