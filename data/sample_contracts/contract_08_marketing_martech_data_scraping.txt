ENTERPRISE DEMAND GENERATION & MARKETING AUTOMATION AGREEMENT
OMNILEADS DEMAND GENERATION AI LLC

This Master Software Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of July 10, 2026, by and between OmniLeads Demand Generation AI LLC, a California limited liability company ("OmniLeads" or "Provider"), and Enterprise Client Corp. ("Customer").

1. ENGAGEMENT AND MARKETING SAAS LICENSING
Provider licenses its proprietary B2B buyer intent intelligence and email sequencing engine to Customer for an Annual Contract Value ("ACV") of Ninety-Five Thousand US Dollars ($95,000.00 USD).

2. INVOICE PAYMENT CYCLES
Customer shall satisfy invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt ("Net 30"). Invoices remaining unpaid after 30 days are subject to late finance charges of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month.

3. TERM AND EXTENSION
The Agreement shall remain in effect for an initial period of twelve (12) calendar months. It shall renew automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party furnishes written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the expiration of the current term. Customer may not terminate for convenience during the active term.

4. SERVICE COMMITMENTS
OmniLeads will endeavor to maintain platform availability at ninety-nine percent (99.0%) during normal business hours. No financial SLA credits or refunds are granted for downtime.

5. RESTRICTIVE LIABILITY PROVISIONS
EXCEPT FOR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, OMNILEADS' TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY FOR ALL CLAIMS AND CAUSES OF ACTION ARISING UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE ABSOLUTELY CAPPED AT TEN THOUSAND US DOLLARS ($10,000.00 USD).

6. DATA RIGHTS AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TRAINING LICENSE
Customer retains base title to its uploaded raw CRM customer contact lists. However, Customer hereby grants OmniLeads a perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to de-identify, vectorize, index, and aggregate all uploaded contact records, intent query telemetry, and message engagement signals to train, benchmark, fine-tune, and improve OmniLeads' predictive B2B targeting algorithms and commercial neural networks.

7. DATA SOVEREIGNTY AND REPLICATION
Customer data may be cached and replicated across globally distributed edge nodes and third-party content distribution networks without geographic restriction. Notice of security breaches will be delivered within forty-eight (48) hours of confirmed incident containment.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be construed under the laws of the State of California.
