CONVERSATIONAL AI & OMNICHANNEL CONTACT CENTER AGREEMENT
VOICEFRONTIER OMNICHANNEL SYSTEMS, INC.

This Master Platform Agreement is entered into on October 5, 2026, by and between VoiceFrontier Omnichannel Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("VoiceFrontier" or "Vendor"), and Enterprise Client Corp. ("Customer").

1. GENERATIVE AI VOICEBOT & MESSAGING SUITE
Vendor provides autonomous AI agent contact center routing and live transcription. Total Annual Contract Value is Two Hundred Ten Thousand US Dollars ($210,000.00 USD).

2. BILLING & FEES
Invoices are payable within twenty (20) days of receipt ("Net 20"). Delinquent amounts are subject to a three percent (3.0%) monthly finance charge.

3. TERM AND CANCELLATION PENALTY
The Initial Term shall be twenty-four (24) months. The contract renews automatically for successive 24-month terms unless notice of non-renewal is delivered at least thirty (30) days prior to the expiration of the current term. Early cancellation by Customer requires payment of a liquidated termination charge of Ten Thousand US Dollars ($10,000.00 USD) plus fifty percent of remaining contract value.

4. SERVICE AVAILABILITY
Vendor targets 99.0% monthly uptime. If uptime drops below 99.0%, Customer's sole remedy shall be a five percent (5%) service credit applied to the next invoice.

5. LIABILITY CAP
Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall be limited to one times (1.0x) the fees paid by Customer in the preceding twelve (12) months ($210,000.00 USD). There are no carve-outs for confidentiality or data security claims.

6. DATA RIGHTS AND ACOUSTIC MODEL TRAINING
Customer grants Vendor a non-exclusive license to analyze, process, and retain anonymized voice recordings, dialogue transcripts, and intent classifications to enhance foundational speech recognition models, acoustic embeddings, and generative language agents.

7. SECURITY BREACH NOTIFICATION
Vendor will notify Customer of any confirmed data breach within five (5) business days of verification.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
