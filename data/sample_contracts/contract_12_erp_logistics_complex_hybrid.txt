ENTERPRISE LOGISTICS & SUPPLY CHAIN CLOUD AGREEMENT
LOGICORE GLOBAL ENTERPRISE SOLUTIONS CORP.

This Cloud Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is dated November 2, 2026, by and between LogiCore Global Enterprise Solutions Corp., a New York corporation ("LogiCore" or "Vendor"), and Enterprise Client Corp. ("Customer").

1. SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT PLATFORM
Vendor licenses its multi-facility ERP warehouse and logistics tracking software. Annual fees are Six Hundred Fifty Thousand US Dollars ($650,000.00 USD), invoiced on a semi-annual schedule.

2. COMMERCIAL TERMS
Invoices are due within thirty (30) days from invoice receipt ("Net 30"). Overdue payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3. DURATION AND RENEWAL
The initial subscription term is thirty-six (36) months. This Agreement automatically extends for consecutive twelve (12) month terms unless non-renewal notice is submitted in writing at least thirty (30) days prior to expiration. Customer may not terminate for convenience.

4. CONFLICTING SERVICE LEVEL OBJECTIVES
(a) Master Agreement Section 4: Vendor targets 99.5% system availability.
(b) Schedule B (Operational Addendum): Notwithstanding any provision in the Master Agreement, uptime figures represent non-binding operational targets only; failure to achieve 99.5% uptime shall not constitute a default or breach of this Agreement, and Vendor shall not be obligated to issue monetary credits, fee abatements, or refunds.

5. LIABILITY CEILING
VENDOR'S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE RESTRICTED TO THE FEES ACTUALLY PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE PRECEDING SIX (6) MONTHS (EQUAL TO 0.5x ACV, OR $325,000.00 USD).

6. DATA RESIDENCY AND REPLICATION
Customer Data may be stored, processed, and replicated across Vendor server clusters situated in North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Vendor will communicate security incident notifications within seventy-two (72) hours.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
