ENTERPRISE DATA PLATFORM AGREEMENT
APEXDATA INTELLIGENCE CORP.

This Enterprise Data Platform Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 ("Effective Date"), by and between ApexData Intelligence Corp., a Delaware corporation ("ApexData" or "Vendor"), and Enterprise Client Corp., a Delaware corporation ("Customer").

1. PLATFORM LICENSING AND FEES
Vendor grants Customer an enterprise subscription to the ApexData Cloud Analytics Engine. The Annual Contract Value ("ACV") is Two Hundred Fifty Thousand US Dollars ($250,000.00 USD), invoiced on an annual billing schedule.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
Customer shall pay all undisputed invoice amounts within forty-five (45) calendar days from receipt of Vendor's invoice ("Net 45"). Vendor offers an early settlement discount of two percent (2.0%) for payments remitted within ten (10) days ("2% Net 10"). Any undisputed late amounts shall bear simple interest at one percent (1.0%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower. Customer reserves the right to withhold disputed fees in good faith pending resolution.

3. TERM, TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE, AND RENEWAL
The initial term shall be twelve (12) months from the Effective Date. The Agreement shall renew for successive one-year terms only upon mutual written agreement, provided that either party gives written notice of renewal intent at least sixty (60) days prior to term expiration. Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time upon thirty (30) days prior written notice to Vendor, whereupon Vendor shall promptly issue a pro-rata refund of any unearned prepaid subscription fees.

4. SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENT AND CREDITS
Vendor warrants that the cloud platform will maintain a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least ninety-nine and ninety-five hundredths percent (99.95%) during each calendar month. In the event uptime falls between 99.0% and 99.95%, Customer shall be entitled to a ten percent (10%) service credit. If uptime falls below 99.0%, Customer shall receive a twenty-five percent (25%) service credit against upcoming invoices, or a cash refund upon contract termination.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION
(a) Except as provided in Section 5(b), each party's maximum aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be capped at two times (2.0x) the total fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim ($500,000.00 USD).
(b) The limitation of liability in Section 5(a) shall NOT apply to: (i) gross negligence or willful misconduct; (ii) breach of Section 7 (Confidentiality and Data Protection); or (iii) Vendor's indemnification obligations regarding third-party intellectual property infringement claims.

6. DATA PROTECTION, ENCRYPTION, AND SECURITY INCIDENTS
Vendor shall implement and maintain rigorous administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. All Customer Data shall be encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and encrypted at rest using AES-256 bit encryption. Customer Data shall reside exclusively within secure data center facilities located inside the continental United States. In the event of a confirmed or suspected Security Incident or unauthorized breach of Customer Data, Vendor shall notify Customer in writing within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.

7. COMPLIANCE AUDITS AND SOC 2 TYPE II
Vendor shall maintain an active AICPA SOC 2 Type II audit report covering Security, Confidentiality, and Availability trust service criteria, and shall provide Customer with a copy of its annual SOC 2 report within fifteen (15) days of issuance. Customer or an independent third-party auditor may conduct an annual security compliance review upon reasonable prior written notice.

8. SUBPROCESSORS
Vendor shall provide Customer with written notice at least thirty (30) days prior to authorizing any new subprocessor. Customer shall have the right to object to such subprocessor on reasonable data security or compliance grounds.

9. CUSTOMER DATA OWNERSHIP AND AI RESTRICTIONS
Customer retains sole and exclusive ownership of all right, title, and interest in and to all Customer Data. Vendor shall NOT use, ingest, or process Customer Data, queries, or derived outputs to train, fine-tune, or benchmark any artificial intelligence, machine learning, or neural network models.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts sitting in New Castle County, Delaware.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement.
